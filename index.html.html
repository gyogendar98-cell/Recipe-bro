Install Native Android App (Zero Play Store Wait)

Recipe Bro includes a built-in Android WebAPK manifest and offline kitchen cache so it installs straight onto your Android home screen with full-screen app behavior.

  1. Open this Recipe Bro link in Google Chrome on your Android phone.
  2. Tap the Three Dots (⋮) menu in the top-right corner.
  3. Select "Add to Home screen" or "Install App".
  4. The Recipe Bro icon appears in your Android app drawer—works offline in your kitchen!
🍎

Install on iPhone & iPad (iOS Native Mode)

Configured with Apple Mobile Web App full-screen status bar and custom Recipe Bro home screen icon.

  1. Open Recipe Bro in Safari on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap the Share icon (⍐) at the bottom center of Safari.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Tap "Add" in the top-right corner to launch Recipe Bro like a native iOS App.

🌐 Live Website + Play Store / App Store Packaging

This single-file codebase powers your Responsive Website immediately. To publish to the Google Play Store (.AAB/.APK) and Apple App Store (.IPA) using this exact file:

  • Google Play Store: Wrap this hosted URL with Trusted Web Activity (Bubblewrap) or Capacitor Android.
  • Apple App Store: Wrap with Capacitor iOS (WKWebView) in Xcode—zero login required!
  • Instant Offline Kitchen Mode: Service Worker caches your recipes & ratings locally on device.
